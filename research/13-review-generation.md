## 6. Volume target & velocity

### 6.1 Target
- **CatLova target 300+ review** (Riset #4) untuk buffer prominens vs PETFIRST (610-670), Dr.Meoww (308-325), Pet Bum (410-466) [ESTIMATED].
- Saat ini 211 → butuh +89 review [VERIFIED] (211 + 89 = 300).

### 6.2 Velocity (konsisten > burst)
- Google favors businesses that receive reviews consistently over time: 5 review/bulan × 12 bulan > 60 review dalam 1 minggu [VERIFIED] AnimalFriendsOS.
- Build sistem yang generate steady, ongoing reviews — bukan burst lalu silence [VERIFIED] AnimalFriendsOS.

### 6.3 Benchmark (sumber berbeda — scope berbeda)
- Top 3 local positions average 47 reviews (truereview.co, semua industri) [ESTIMATED] (single source, marketing blog).
- Top-3 posisi pet business rata-rata 200+ review (Petboost 2025 GBP research) [ESTIMATED] (single source, marketing blog).
- 31% consumers only consider businesses with 4.5+ stars [ESTIMATED] truereview.co (single source, marketing blog).
- Review signals ~20% of Google's Local Pack ranking algorithm [ESTIMATED] truereview.co (single source, marketing blog).
- CatLova sudah 211 review (>> 47, ~ sebanding 200+) → sudah kuat; target 300+ untuk buffer [ESTIMATED].

### 6.4 Math contoh (GroomBoard — asumsi ilustrasi)
- 120 klien belum pernah diminta review, spread 6 minggu = 20 ask/minggu.
- Konversi 20% → 4 review/minggu → 24 review dalam 6 minggu.
- Steady state: 3 klien baru/minggu × 20% = 0.6 review/minggu ≈ 2-3/bulan.
- **Pelajaran:** sweep saja tidak cukup; butuh sweep + steady + loyal milestone + QR untuk catch up [VERIFIED] GroomBoard.

Catatan: angka 120/20%/3 adalah asumsi ilustrasi GroomBoard, bukan data CatLova. CatLova active client count [UNKNOWN].

## 8. Handling review negatif

| Step | Aksi | Label |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Respons dalam 24 jam (speed shows you care) | [VERIFIED] AnimalFriendsOS |
| 2 | Thank them for feedback (bahkan jika tidak setuju) | [VERIFIED] AnimalFriendsOS, GroomBoard |
| 3 | Acknowledge experience ("We're sorry your visit didn't meet expectations") | [VERIFIED] AnimalFriendsOS |
| 4 | Take offline (telepon/WA) untuk resolve | [VERIFIED] AnimalFriendsOS, GroomBoard |
| 5 | Jangan argue publik, jangan blame klien, jangan reveal detail pet/account | [VERIFIED] AnimalFriendsOS, GroomBoard, Paragon |
| 6 | Keep it brief (calm, professional, caring) | [VERIFIED] Paragon, GroomBoard |

- Review yang tidak setuju bukan otomatis policy breach; Google reporting route untuk review yang break rules [VERIFIED] WoofSpark.
- Jangan pernah refund/fix/future booking tergantung pada klien mengubah/menghapus review [VERIFIED] WoofSpark, Google policy.

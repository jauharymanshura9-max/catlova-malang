### 1.2 Tone of voice (ramah, profesional, empatik)
- **Prinsip (Madegooddesigns):** "Talk like a knowledgeable friend, not a corporate brochure and not a baby-talking cartoon." [VERIFIED].
- **Lead with the pet, not the transaction:** "kucing kamu pulang wangi & tenang" beats "layanan grooming profesional" [VERIFIED] madegooddesigns.
- **Specific about care:** concrete details (cara handle kucing galak, rutinitas kebersihan, update WA) build trust lebih dari kata "passionate"/"premium" [VERIFIED].
- **Consistent voice:** tone di signage, website, IG, WA harus sama orang [VERIFIED].
- **Match warmth to stakes:** grooming = ramah & playful boleh; penitipan/medis = lebih tenang & reassuring [VERIFIED].
- **CatLova voice guide (usulan):**
  - "Kami yang datang ke kamu." (antar-jemput) — [ESTIMATED]
  - "Satu kucing, satu perhatian." (personal) — [ESTIMATED]
  - "Pulang wangi, pulang tenang." (outcome) — [ESTIMATED]
  - Hindari: jargon teknis ("de-matting", "hydro-bath") tanpa penjelasan [VERIFIED] pagekiss.

## 3. Tipografi

### 3.1 Rekomendasi (berbasis Madegooddesigns + Petoria + Serenity)
- **Display/heading (personality):** Quicksand (rounded, instantly friendly) atau Nunito (rounded sans, flexible, true italic) — [VERIFIED] madegooddesigns. Alternatif premium: serif editorial dengan italic accent (Petoria: "oversized headlines with italic serif accents") [VERIFIED] rockysoft.ca.
- **Body:** Inter atau Source Sans (neutral, highly legible) [VERIFIED] madegooddesigns.
- **Pairing rule:** satu personality face + satu neutral body; dua personality face bertarung [VERIFIED] madegooddesigns.

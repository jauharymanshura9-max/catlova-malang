## 3. Format yang bekerja (dari web search 2026)

### 3.1 Before/after reveal (transformation)
- "Single most shareable asset" (Groomer News) [VERIFIED].
- 15-30 detik Reels/TikTok [VERIFIED] Groomer News + ZSky.
- Dramatic transformation (matted → clean, overgrown → styled) paling tinggi engagement [VERIFIED] Daily Groomer.
- Same angle, same light, plain backdrop agar coat jadi fokus [VERIFIED] DataLatte.
- Reveal tiba awal (hook cepat) [VERIFIED] DataLatte.
- Satu viral before/after bisa bawa 10-20 klien baru [ESTIMATED] tryteddy.

### 3.2 Process clip (satisfying)
- Clippers gliding, brush finishing, scissoring face, real sound (no music) [VERIFIED] DataLatte + Fluffday.
- Easy to batch (film while working) [VERIFIED] DataLatte.
- 10-20 detik [VERIFIED] Fluffday.

### 3.3 Breed/coat explainer (edukasi)
- "What a doodle coat needs between grooms", "Why we do not shave double coats" [VERIFIED] DataLatte.
- Fewer views than reveal tapi attract serious owners [VERIFIED] DataLatte.

### 3.4 First-groom & nervous-dog story
- Puppy's first visit filmed gently → trust builder [VERIFIED] DataLatte.
- "Visit 1, visit 3, visit 6" series (Fluffday) [VERIFIED].

### 3.5 Local hooks
- "Grooming spots near [neighbourhood]", clip of street outside salon [VERIFIED] DataLatte.
- "Best dog walk in [your town]" [VERIFIED] Fluffday.

### 3.6 Lain-lain (Fluffday 20 ide)

- POV: your first groom (dog eye level + captions as dog thoughts) [VERIFIED].
- A day in the life (timestamps) [VERIFIED].
- How much fur came off (bag + weigh + guess) [VERIFIED].
- Answer a comment (reply with Reel) [VERIFIED].
- Myth busting (shaking head + explaining) [VERIFIED].
- Meet the team in 15 seconds [VERIFIED].
- The collection reaction (owner seeing dog first time, ask first) [VERIFIED].
- Post bath zoomies (rate out of 10) [VERIFIED].
- What's in my grooming bag/kit [VERIFIED].
- Brushed at home vs not (same breed, 8 weeks) [VERIFIED].
- Senior dog care (breaks, mats, softer table) [VERIFIED].
- Behind the scenes clean down (towel mountain, reset) [VERIFIED].

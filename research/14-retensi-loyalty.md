## 4. ABK Grooming 10-stage retention system

[VERIFIED] ABK Grooming (https://www.abkgrooming.com/blogs/grooming/customer-retention-strategies-for-pet-groomers-booking-loyalty-strategies)

1. Record the salon's current retention rate.
2. Remove unnecessary booking friction.
3. Confirm the service and appointment expectations.
4. Record the pet's preferences and grooming history.
5. Deliver a consistent, personalised appointment.
6. Send a grooming report.
7. Collect feedback and resolve concerns.
8. Recommend the next appointment.
9. Reward valuable client behaviour.
10. Contact inactive clients before they disappear permanently.

Prinsip: "treated as an operating procedure, not as separate marketing campaigns" [VERIFIED] ABK Grooming.

CatLova status: stage 1-10 [UNKNOWN] (belum ada sistem). Rekomendasi: mulai stage 4 (record pet preferences/history — buku/catatan manual) + stage 8 (recommend next appointment at checkout) + stage 10 (contact inactive) [ESTIMATED].

## 5. Loyalty models

[VERIFIED] ABK Grooming (tabel model)

| Model | Best For | Main Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Visit-based rewards | Small and simple salons | Rewards all visits equally |
| Spend-based points | Mixed services and retail | Can become complicated |
| Membership | Regular maintenance clients | Creates fulfilment liability |
| Prepaid packages | Predictable visit frequency | Over-discounting |
| Referral rewards | Client acquisition | Duplicate or invalid referrals |
| VIP tiers | High-value clients | Benefits must remain meaningful |

### Rekomendasi CatLova (berbasis ABK Grooming + Leat + AnimalFriendsOS)
- **Mulai:** visit-based (reward after 5-6 completed visits) + referral credit + priority booking + birthday add-on + occasional service upgrades [ESTIMATED] ABK Grooming ("A small salon can begin with: reward after five or six completed visits, referral credit, priority booking for frequent clients, birthday add-on, occasional service upgrades").
- **Hindari:** large permanent discounts ("should not depend on large permanent discounts") [VERIFIED] ABK Grooming.
- **Membership (fase 2):** monthly base service (1 full groom/bulan) + perks (discounted add-ons, priority booking, free nail trims between grooms), price 10-15% di bawah a la carte [ESTIMATED] AnimalFriendsOS. Test profitability sebelum launch [VERIFIED] ABK Grooming.

By 20 days, the client has had time to settle into being a satisfied customer, the dog looks good, and they have probably already mentioned your salon to at least one person organically. Formalizing that with a referral link at exactly this point catches the momentum without feeling premature." [VERIFIED] Spokk.
